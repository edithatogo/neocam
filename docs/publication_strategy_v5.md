# Publication strategy — 28 October 2026

## Recommended route
First submission: **Journal of Telemedicine and Telecare**, as an Original Article. Telemedicine and e-Health is the closest topical alternative; Cost Effectiveness and Resource Allocation is particularly suitable for a methods-forward paper. Australian Health Review is preferable when Australian commissioning impact takes priority. JMIR is a stretch target, not a predicted acceptance probability. Current publisher pages, formats and fee verification are in `evidence/v5/journal_targets.csv`.

## Proposed title
**Choosing neonatal teleconsultation technology for regional hospitals: a five-strategy economic model and uncertainty analysis in Queensland**

## Defensible contribution
This is not the first neonatal telemedicine economic model. The contribution is the explicit comparison of five complete service configurations, contemporary but uncertain technology/resource costs, new-investment versus retained-equipment decisions, correlated uncertainty across Queensland sites, and reproducible exact decision boundaries. The four-to-five Atherton episodes support local pathway description, not an effectiveness estimate. Telephone consultation occurs in both arms. OBSBOT is a proposed complete kit, not an independent camera-only treatment or an approved procurement choice.

## Main manuscript versus supplement
Keep the main paper focused on the decision, evidence inputs, five options, pilot/state results, incumbent decisions, key one-/two-way boundaries, resource/funding distinction and limitations. Use four principal tables (options/assumptions; evidence/inputs; comparative results; decision scenarios) and three or four figures (paired uncertainty; key decision surface; incumbent comparison; one-way drivers). Put all grids, variance decomposition, surrogate learning diagnostics, source register, model code, technical tests and reporting checklist in the supplement. Machine learning is explanatory and secondary, not the clinical innovation.

## Readiness requirements
- Audit the critical clinical/economic papers in full text and document transportability and study overlap; current targeted evidence is not a completed systematic review.
- Retain source price year, indexing, marginal-cost fraction, complete-kit scope and uncertainty. Missing invoices or formal expert elicitation do not prohibit a model-based study, but must not be represented as verified inputs.
- Obtain independent clinical/economic structural and implementation review. Local trial evidence or a new RCT is not a prerequisite for this existing-evidence modelling paper.
- Report all strategies, correlated PSA, effect structures, incumbent/sunk-cost assumptions, feasibility restrictions and sample-design limits. Conditional equal video effectiveness is not demonstrated equivalence; no proven cost-minimisation or QALY claim.
- Describe input-only clustering as unstable under the tested design. Report held-out classifier accuracy AND regret; cost-prediction R-squared alone failed to identify decisions reliably. No synthetic patient subgroup claim.
- Complete reproducible connected toolchain acceptance and archive an immutable code/data release only after review. The current repository is a private working handoff; it is not a public deposition or hosted-CI pass.
- Use CHEERS 2022 with a separate budget-impact appendix. Resolve ethics/governance status with the institution, authorship, funding, conflicts, data rights and AI-use disclosure. Do not invent an ethics exemption, approvals or coauthor agreement.
- Exclude the full ELC pack, corporate templates/artwork and any restricted records from public research deposit unless permission covers redistribution. A cleaned source/derived-data release can be prepared separately.

# Scientific validation and publication acceptance

This is an early decision model, not a validated clinical prediction system. Technical checks and simulated role reviews are author verification, not independent review. No local empirical model is fitted to the 4–5 Atherton episodes. The source of each assumption remains in the existing evidence register; uncertainty distributions are not automatically posterior estimates.

## Executed scientific consistency checks
- Current configuration reaches real calculation and export entry points, including a temporary altered-price scenario.
- Absolute pathway counts conserve the cohort; failed video falls back to telephone; critical cases receive no unsupported avoidance credit.
- Fiscal/patient allocations reconcile without creating societal resources.
- Mixed estate uses the same known ownership for economic retention and cash purchases; replacement dates are explicit assumptions.
- Prior-predictive tables expose implied transfer, local-care, rescue and workload distributions for each strategy/network. These are model-implied consequences, not observed outcome estimates.
- Shared parameter draws remain paired across strategies/sites. Fixed expected-choice value of perspective remains distinct from draw-wise perfect information.

## Clinical/economic review protocol — not executed independently
Reviewer records: identity/role, conflicts, source commit, configuration hash, date, findings and resolution. Reviewers assess the *outputs*, not just whether the code runs. No sign-off may be inferred from a blank form.
1. Neonatal/retrieval reviewer: compare implied immediate/late transfers, local retention and critical strata with plausible capability and workflow; review the eventual-versus-initial-transfer endpoint.
2. Economic reviewer: check hospital component attribution, avoidable retrieval shares, repeated contacts, mixed ownership, replacement and hub capacity; keep standard prices separate from marginal resources and cash.
3. Independent technical reviewer: hand-calculate selected cohorts/costs and inspect null/extreme inputs; do not reuse the same helper as both implementation and oracle.
4. Consumer/rural reviewer: address family separation, access and cultural safety without pretending monetary proxies encompass all consequences.

## Four critical original full texts still open
Haynes 2020 transfer-effect paper; Armfield 2012; Yoo 2022; Thao 2022. Record full bibliographic identity, source/version, recruitment/exclusion criteria, endpoint timing, effect adjustment, price year, component-cost coverage, funding perspective and dependencies/shared study cohorts. Do not count derivative economic models as independent effect studies. Abstract access is not full-text verification.

## Bounded follow-on scenarios (not invented in this release)
- Explicit contacts per infant: clinical outcomes apply once per infant; additional contacts add workload without multiplying transfer opportunities. Requires a clear incident denominator and baseline contact convention.
- Mixed technology deployment: compare a few clinically eligible policies (retain installed Panda; shared existing mobile unit; new kit where approved), not exhaustive 33-site optimisation. Aggregate hub workload before applying capacity steps.
- Clinical appropriateness constraint: extra appropriate transfer may be beneficial; report safety/access alongside costs. Do not infer device equivalence from a low-cost reference arm.
- External comparisons: use compatible populations/endpoints and genuinely independent samples. Check prior-predictive distributions before calibration; do not tune priors to force positive savings.
- Long-term health: age-specific survival/utilities, future costs and caregiver spillovers remain separate. No conversion of disability weight to utility or addition of QALYs to DALYs.

## Publication and privacy acceptance
Complete CHEERS reporting plus AdViSHE/TECH-VER-style validation records; these are not certifications. Verify current journal and institutional publishing agreement at submission/acceptance. Confirm authorship and governance, funder/vendor conflicts, data availability and source redistribution permissions. A private working repo may contain corporate templates; public release requires an explicit file-level allowlist. Gitleaks detects secrets, not all patient information or licensed/organisational confidentiality.

## Stop expanding the toolchain
No new orchestrator, mutation framework or learned surrogate. Freeze one tested research environment and assess updates separately. Reconcile affected tracks, not every historical checklist on every edit. The next acceptance is a reproducible current release, not a larger test count.

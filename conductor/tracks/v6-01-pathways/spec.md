# V6 scope: explicit care pathways and incremental hub resources

User authorised the recommended structural repair, evidence verification and validation. Keep the five configurations and existing historical evidence; do not pretend new empirical effectiveness or local cost data exist.

## Design decisions
- Preserve v5 numerical code as explicitly historical for reproducibility. The active v6 evaluator must not call the legacy `_kernel` with the site-count trigger. Add one small vectorised pathway kernel and one analysis driver, not an event simulator.
- Published transfer odds ratios concern recorded transfers, not necessarily immediate transfer decisions. The primary structural hypothesis applies the effect to eventual transfer; decompose this into immediate and delayed transfers. Do not apply a second transfer-reduction penalty to the same endpoint. A separate initial-decision interpretation and excess-rescue scenarios test endpoint uncertainty.
- Report absolute immediate transfer, initially local, delayed rescue and final local-care counts for telephone and each video option. Every draw and stratum conserves the cohort. Video failure follows the telephone branch.
- Price non-overlapping local-only, origin-before-transfer, receiving-hospital and extra pre-rescue care explicitly. Verified national price/weight text is a benchmark; clinical mappings and marginal fractions are analyst assumptions, not grouped patient records. Hospital cost and funding are separate; missing QEP stays missing.
- Replace the number-of-sites rule with incremental video workload. Preserve the reference AUD75/call staff resource allowance by splitting it explicitly into local/coordination AUD25 and hub AUD50 (15 minutes at AUD200/hour). This allocation is assumed. Opportunity cost applies to all incremental hub minutes, even where cash capacity is spare. Cash capacity is modelled separately as blocks beyond an explicit spare-hours allowance; never add cash blocks to the hourly resource valuation.
- Annual economic capital and undiscounted cash purchases/replacements are distinct. Incumbent equipment has a specified remaining life; purchases are not free. No automatic savings cash claim.
- Remove ML from the active analysis and publication narrative. Archive earlier diagnostics; no new ML dependency or feature. Keep cost-consequence scope and a limited family-cost extension.

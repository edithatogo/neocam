# Product guidelines
Use existing evidence and explicit assumptions for neonatal service investment, not clinical prediction.
Report financial resources, budget transfers, patient effects and health outcomes separately.
Never equate fewer transfers, a low-cost device, a green unit test or simulated review with better care.
Preserve privacy and source rights; no patient-level data or credentials in this project.
The exact transparent model is authoritative. No new orchestrator, dataframe engine or learned surrogate.
Keep historical results; generate new, labelled releases without overwriting them.
